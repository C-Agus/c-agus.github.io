Senior capstone · Aug 2026 – present

Grand Exchange Strategy Backtester In progress

A web-based backtesting tool, built with a five-person team for FSU's Software Engineering Capstone, that replays user-defined trading strategies against historical price data from Old School RuneScape's Grand Exchange, a player-driven marketplace with real supply and demand.

  • Stack: JavaScript, HTML/CSS
  • Data: OSRS Wiki Real-time Prices API
  • Team: 5 developers
Team repository on GitHub

The idea

Why a backtester?

In finance, a backtest answers one question: "If I had followed this strategy in the past, how would it have done?" Traders run strategies against historical data before risking real money.

The Grand Exchange is a surprisingly good place to practice this. Thousands of items trade constantly, prices move with real player supply and demand, and the OSRS Wiki publishes the price history through a free public API. That gives us realistic market data without the cost or licensing of real financial feeds.

The core feature isn't recommending trades. It's letting a user define a strategy and replay it against historical prices to see how it would have performed.

Design

Scope and architecture

  • Static, backend-free front end. The app runs entirely in the browser with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which keeps hosting simple and removes a whole class of server problems.
  • Guest access. No accounts or stored personal data, so there's nothing sensitive to protect.
  • Live data source. Price history comes straight from the OSRS Wiki Real-time Prices API.

Our proposal was accepted in September 2026, and the team is building it in milestones through the fall semester.
